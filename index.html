Product Growth at Exa · San Francisco

I work on product growth at Exa, an applied AI lab building web search for AI agents. I've spent most of my career helping new products find their market. Working at an AI lab is turning me back into a builder. A good chunk of my job now runs on agents I wrote myself.

Exa

Within a few years, AI agents will search the web more than people do, and Google wasn't built to serve them. Exa was. Cursor, Cognition, and HubSpot already run on it, and I spend my days with the teams bringing it into production. Will, Exa's CEO, wrote a vision letter about that bet.

Building with agents · Aug 2026

How much of a product growth and go-to-market job can you hand over to your own agents? So far, more than I expected. What stays human is the judgment. What to delegate, what to verify, what to ship, and what still deserves a real conversation. And ideally, all of it without burying anyone in slop.
