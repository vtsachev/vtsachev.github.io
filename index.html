Product Growth at Exa · San Francisco

Thesis

Within a few years, AI agents will search the web more than people do. That is Exa's bet, and mine. Exa is an applied AI lab building web search for AI agents. Cursor, Cognition, and HubSpot use it. Much of my own job already runs on agents I build.

Against slop

Agents make it cheap to produce more: more research, more drafts, more words. Left alone, they bury the reader in made-up facts, off-topic writing, and volume nobody asked for. Nobody gets that to zero. Most of my effort with AI goes into getting close.

Now · Aug 2026

Partnering with teams building AI products as they bring Exa into production. Rebuilding my own workflow as a set of agents, from research to follow-through. The interesting problem is deciding what to delegate, what to verify, and what still deserves a conversation.

Background

I grew up in Bulgaria, wrote code at SAP, moved to Silicon Valley, and found my way into go-to-market after an MBA at Berkeley Haas. Since then: first sales hire for a new AI product at Google Cloud (3,000+ customers, $20M ARR), first sales leader at Triplebyte (one to twenty people, zero to eight figures), and early-stage go-to-market at Trunk.io. At Exa, the two halves of that path finally run together: engineering and growth.
