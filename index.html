Product Growth at Exa · San Francisco

I work on product growth at Exa, an applied AI lab building web search for AI agents. I've spent my career helping new products find their market. Working at an AI lab is turning me back into a builder: much of my job now runs on agents I write.

Exa

Within a few years, AI agents will search the web more than people do, and Google wasn't built to serve them. Exa was. Cursor, Cognition, and HubSpot already run on it; my days go to the teams bringing it into production. The vision letter is a two-minute read, and worth it.

Building with agents · Aug 2026

My working setup is an experiment: how much of one job can a person hand to agents they wrote themselves. So far the answer is a lot. The part that stays human is judgment: what to delegate, what to verify, what to ship, and what still deserves a conversation, all without burying anyone in slop.
